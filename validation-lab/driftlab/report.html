Interpretation and calibration
Compare one parameter at a time on the same input. Inspect median shifts, recovery exclusions, warm-up and threshold sensitivity. Lower or smoother drift does not establish accuracy. Use independently selected steady segments before calling a rejection false. Review long rides for bucket split error, power changes for effort confounding, missing seconds and pauses for replay uncertainty. No recommendation to change production thresholds is justified by an empty archive.
